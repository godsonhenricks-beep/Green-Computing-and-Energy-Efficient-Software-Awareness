# Ethics and Responsible AI

- **Cite all statistics** with source, year and link; check the original, not the AI summary.
- **Avoid greenwashing:** no claims such as "carbon neutral" without evidence.
- **State measurement limits:** our energy values are estimates (runtime × assumed power); results vary by hardware.
- **AI's own footprint:** AI tools use energy; we limited prompts and reused outputs.
- **Disclose AI use:** list tools and what each produced.
- **Human review:** all AI outputs were checked before inclusion.
